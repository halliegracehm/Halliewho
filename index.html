Caption rules

Shape

A hook your listener feels, then 🎙️ New episode out now. 💜

Always end with a CTA

"Drop a 💜 if this hit." · "Save this if you needed it." · "Send this to someone who needs it."

Keep it for everyone

No em dashes. No "sisters" openers. Gender-neutral, for anyone carrying something.
